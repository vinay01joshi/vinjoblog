# 7 Best Tech Gadgets of August 2026

### Deep Dive & Technical Insights

#### 1. piBrick Pocket-CM5

![piBrick Pocket-CM5 Front](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/06/this-172-raspberry-pi-handheld-doubles-as-a-usb-keyboard/pibrick-pocket-cm5-10.jpg)
![piBrick Pocket-CM5 Angle](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/06/this-172-raspberry-pi-handheld-doubles-as-a-usb-keyboard/pibrick-pocket-cm5-02.jpg)

Built by Ahmad Amarullah on a custom PCB designed around the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5, the [piBrick Pocket-CM5](https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/06/03/this-172-raspberry-pi-handheld-doubles-as-a-usb-keyboard/) is an open-source handheld costing around $172 in parts. Arriving at compact dimensions ($80 \times 145 \times 19.6\text{ mm}$), it features a 3.92-inch AMOLED display running at $1080 \times 1240$ resolution at 90Hz, covered by Asahi tempered glass. Full-size and micro-HDMI outputs allow the pocket device to drive external monitors directly.

The built-in BBQ20 physical keyboard incorporates a BlackBerry-derived trackpad, complemented by side rotary encoders and five programmable buttons. Hardware specs include a 5,000mAh LiPo battery, NVMe SSD support (2230 or 2242 form factors), and USB 3.0 Type-A and Type-C ports. Crucially, its **USB-HID mode** allows the device to act as an external USB keyboard and trackpad when plugged into any computer.

##### What We Like:
- **USB-HID Mode**: Functions as a standalone input device for external machines.
- **High-Spec Display**: 90Hz AMOLED panel in a DIY pocket form factor.

##### What We Dislike:
- **Assembly Required**: Requires manual component sourcing, PCB manufacturing, and 3D printing.
- **Learning Curve**: The compact BBQ20 keyboard layout takes time to adapt to for long typing sessions.

#### 2. OrigamiSwift Folding Mouse

![OrigamiSwift Flat](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2025/07/clever-gadgets-tools-to-upgrade-your-remote-work-setup/10_best_clever_tools_remote_workers_yanko_design_02.jpg)
![OrigamiSwift Formed](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/08/tech-gadgets-of-aug-2026/7_best_tech_gadgets_august_yanko_design_hero.jpg)

Designed by Horace Lam, the OrigamiSwift is a Bluetooth mouse that folds flat to 0.18 inches—thinner than most physical notepads—and snaps into a full-sized ergonomic shape in under half a second using structural origami geometry. Weighing only 40 grams, it connects via Bluetooth 5.2 and utilizes a 4,000 CPI HD infrared sensor capable of tracking up to 30 inches per second.

The rigid triangular structure absorbs daily pressure without bending, and soft-click buttons provide quiet feedback. Battery performance reaches up to three months on a single USB-C charge. It supports macOS, iPadOS, Windows, and Android natively without additional driver installations.

*   **Price**: $85.00 ([Buy Now](https://shop.yankodesign.com/collections/best-sellers/products/origamiswift-folding-mouse))

##### What We Like:
- **Battery Efficiency**: Up to 3 months of use from a single USB-C charge.
- **Ultra-Lightweight**: At 40g, it offers extreme portability for travel setups.

##### What We Dislike:
- **CPI Ceiling**: 4,000 CPI maximum might not suit ultra-high-resolution or multi-display power users.
- **Availability**: Limited stock availability.

#### 3. Divoom FlowToo

![Divoom FlowToo Front](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/the-99-gadget-that-clears-3-devices-off-your-nightstand/divoom-flowtoo-bedside-speaker-alarm-clock-05.jpg)
![Divoom FlowToo Display](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/the-99-gadget-that-clears-3-devices-off-your-nightstand/divoom-flowtoo-bedside-speaker-alarm-clock-04.jpg)

The [Divoom FlowToo](https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/07/01/the-99-gadget-that-clears-3-devices-off-your-nightstand/) ($99.99) consolidates a Bluetooth speaker, white noise generator, and smart alarm clock into a single bedside unit. Powered by a 10W amplifier paired with a 45mm full-range driver, it offers clear audio output across ambient and music playback modes. It comes pre-loaded with over 90 ambient soundscapes managed via the companion Divoom smartphone application.

Its 2.26-inch integrated smart display displays custom clock faces, audio visualizers, sleep animations, and mood lighting. The wake-up routine gently increases light and volume levels over time rather than relying on abrupt alarms.

##### What We Like:
- **Audio Quality**: 10W full-range driver delivers solid audio quality for a nightstand device.
- **Gentle Alarms**: Gradual light-and-sound alarm sequence provides a better wake-up experience.

##### What We Dislike:
- **Specialization Trade-off**: Dedicated standalone speakers or white-noise machines outclass it in their single categories.
- **App Reliance**: Core functionality customization depends on the smartphone app.

#### 4. Portable CD Cover Player

![Portable CD Cover Player Case](https://shop.yankodesign.com/cdn/shop/files/portable_CD_cover_player_18_1400x.jpg?v=1702813396)
![Portable CD Cover Player Open](https://shop.yankodesign.com/cdn/shop/files/portable_CD_cover_player_11_1400x.jpg?v=1702813396)

Designed to match the square dimensions of a classic jewel case or CD sleeve, the Portable CD Cover Player makes album artwork the physical centerpiece of digital-analogue audio listening. It caters to users seeking deliberate album-length playback away from algorithm-driven streaming services.

By combining retro physical media support with a modern, compact chassis, the device provides portable hi-fi audio tailored for CD collectors.

*   **Price**: $199.00 ([Buy Now](https://shop.yankodesign.com/collections/best-sellers/products/portable-cd-cover-player))

##### What We Like:
- **Tactile Design**: Highlights full album cover art during physical media playback.
- **Focused Listening**: Promotes distraction-free, front-to-back album playback.

##### What We Dislike:
- **Niche Market**: Requires a pre-existing physical CD library to justify the price point.
- **Low Inventory**: Limited stock available at launch.

#### 5. Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 S-Pen](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/galaxy-z-fold-8-july-22-date-confirmed-s-pen-returns/samsung_galaxy_zfold_8_s_pen_1.jpeg)
![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Angle](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/galaxy-z-fold-8-july-22-date-confirmed-s-pen-returns/samsung_galaxy_zfold_8_s_pen_3.jpeg)

Samsung’s [Galaxy Z Fold 8 line](https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/07/13/galaxy-z-fold-8-july-22-date-confirmed-s-pen-returns/) splits into three distinct variants:
1. **Standard Fold 8**: 4.1mm thin when unfolded.
2. **Fold 8 Wide**: 3.9mm thin profile.
3. **Fold 8 Ultra**: Re-introduces S Pen support alongside a titanium-and-carbon-fiber chassis and a 5,000mAh battery.

The material choices on the Ultra allow for digitizer integration without adding excessive chassis thickness, while offering the largest battery capacity seen in the series since the Z Fold 3.

##### What We Like:
- **Ultra Features**: S Pen support returns to the Ultra alongside a larger 5,000mAh battery.
- **Diverse Options**: Tiered model structures cater separately to users wanting thinness versus productivity features.

##### What We Dislike:
- **Feature Gating**: S Pen support is restricted exclusively to the top-tier Ultra model.
- **Multiple SKUs**: Splitting the Fold lineup into three versions may complicate choice for consumers.

#### 6. Google Home Speaker

![Google Home Speaker Front](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/06/auto-draft/google_home_speaker_2026_1.jpeg)
![Google Home Speaker Details](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/06/auto-draft/google_home_speaker_2026_4.jpeg)

Marking Google’s first standalone smart speaker release since the 2020 Nest Audio, the [new Google Home Speaker](https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/06/03/after-6-years-google-finally-remembered-to-launch-a-new-smart-speaker-this-time-with-gemini-built-in/) centres around native **Gemini AI** integration. The hardware features a rounded profile with soft fabric finishes, 360-degree audio drivers, accelerated local processing for faster voice responses, and a redesigned LED ring indicator to signal system activity.

##### What We Like:
- **Native Gemini AI**: Enables fluid, conversational interactions beyond traditional voice assistant commands.
- **Hardware Upgrade**: Updated audio processing offers a clear leap over 2020-era Nest units.

##### What We Dislike:
- **Ecosystem Uncertainty**: Inconsistent long-term smart home hardware strategies raise support questions.
- **AI-Centric Focus**: Marketing prioritizes AI integration over dedicated acoustic breakthroughs.

#### 7. Inkleaf Foldable Dual-Screen E Ink Notebook

![Inkleaf Open](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/inkleafs-foldable-dual-screen-e-ink-notebook-lets-you-read-and-write-at-the-same-time/inkleaf-dual-screen-eink-notebook-07.jpg)
![Inkleaf Angle View](https://www.yankodesign.com/images/design_news/2026/07/inkleafs-foldable-dual-screen-e-ink-notebook-lets-you-read-and-write-at-the-same-time/inkleaf-dual-screen-eink-notebook-02.jpg)

Designed by Kameron Yu, the [Inkleaf Dual-Screen E Ink Notebook](https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/07/13/inkleafs-foldable-dual-screen-e-ink-notebook-lets-you-read-and-write-at-the-same-time/) incorporates two 10.3-inch Carta panels on a precision-machined 180-degree hinge, enabling side-by-side reading and note-taking. 

When folded, the cast aluminum body measures 8.4mm thick and weighs 480 grams. Unfolded, each side measures 4.2mm thin. The custom Android OS natively opens PDF, EPUB, MOBI, and TXT files, backed by AI-driven handwriting indexing for full-notebook search functionality.

*   **Price**: ~$450.00 (Shipping August 2026)

##### What We Like:
- **Dual-Screen Workflow**: Eliminates app-switching friction by keeping reading materials and notes open at once.
- **Ultra-Slim Profile**: At 4.2mm per panel, it is thinner unfolded than most single-screen E-Ink tablets.

##### What We Dislike:
- **High Entry Price**: $450 requires a strong commitment to an E Ink writing workflow.
- **Refresh Rates**: Android apps on E Ink displays remain limited by typical panel refresh latency.
