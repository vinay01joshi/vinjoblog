## Software Landscape & Selection Criteria

For anyone who wants to bring a design to life, computer-aided design (CAD) is an essential building block in the 3D printing workflow. Selecting CAD software can seem like a daunting prospect, especially with many options costing large sums of money. Fortunately, there are plenty of free options all aimed at generating 3D models.

Critical to its usefulness is how easy CAD software makes it to interact with the 3D printing process, particularly the ability to generate 3D models that can be saved in the STL or OBJ format. These formats are easily converted into instructions for 3D printers via slicer software.

To simplify your choice, the best free CAD tools are organized below by user skill level—from absolute beginners to advanced parametric modeling experts.

---

## Beginner Software Options

> Pro-Tip: When starting out with 3D modeling for 3D printing, focus on mastering simple geometric unions and subtractions before moving on to complex sculpting or parametric constraints.

### Tinkercad

![Tinkercad is simple to use but capable of sophisticated 3D design work](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102750/tinkercad-is-simple-and-easy-enough-but-capable-of-tinkercad-via-twitter-210809_download.jpg)

Tinkercad is one of the most popular educational 3D modeling tools. Its interface and featured tools are friendly and easy to use, making the 3D design process fun for kids, hobbyists, educators, and above all, beginners.

Regarding 3D printing, users can export designed objects in STL or OBJ formats. Another great feature of Tinkercad is that it’s completely browser-based, so there are no compatibility issues and minimal system requirements.

The platform offers plenty of learning resources and projects, and it’s especially useful for those learning or even teaching 3D modeling. Tinkercad also hosts a user gallery where 3D models from other users can be viewed and even copied for further customization.

* **Operating System:** Browser-based
* **License Type:** Free to use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Constructive Solid Geometry / Block Modeling
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, SVG

### 3D Slash

![As easy as playing a game](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/15132401/software-3D-printing-3D-Slash-1.jpg)

3D Slash is an incredibly easy-to-use 3D modeling tool built with non-designers and children in mind. 3D printing inspired the creation of this software back in 2014, with its creator, Sylvain Huet, wanting to provide simple 3D modeling software the average Joe would have no issues getting to grips with. It’s a basic program with a basic set of tools, but users can model shapes quickly and easily, no matter their starting skill level.

Multi-part models can be modeled with different colors and then exported by color. The modeling program itself is so light it can run on a Raspberry Pi, but it still manages to offer a platform with enough features to be useful for producing simple models for printing.

3D Slash is offered in both free and paid versions. The free version is limited to the web application, a reduced color palette, and queued exports. All of the subscription plans offer modeling in a higher resolution, a full-color palette, immediate exports, and functional use of the 3D Slash app.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS, Linux, Raspberry Pi OS (or browser-based)
* **License Type:** Free limited use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Block-based / Voxel Modeling
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ

### SelfCAD

![SelfCAD's UI is easy to navigate, and mimics Fusion 360's one.](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/01081757/selfcads-ui-is-easy-to-navigate-and-mimics-fusio-selfcad-220630_download.jpg)

SelfCAD is a distinctive, fully-fledged environment that supports CAD model design, free-form sculpting, rendering, animation, and even 3D slicing. The entire package can be accessed either online via a web interface or offline by installing it locally.

Newcomers and occasional designers will appreciate the straightforward approach to modeling, and the included interactive tutorials are a great way to get started. As far as approachability goes, SelfCAD is one of the better options out there.

The interface is slim but functional and is unlikely to confuse the user by offering too many tools to choose from. From a pure 3D printing perspective, the integrated slicing engine supports a decent range of printers, with the option to customize the profiles too. Keeping processes all under the same roof allows for a tidier workflow, and that’s always an appealing option.

It offers a basic free plan with access to basic features such as 3D modeling tools, shape generators, modifiers, and built-in slicer.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS (or browser-based)
* **License Type:** Free limited use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Mesh & Free-form Modeling / Direct CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, integrated slicing output

## Intermediate Software Options

> Pro-Tip: Always double-check your exported mesh integrity in file repair applications or slicers before printing to ensure non-manifold edges or inverted normals don't cause print failures.

### FreeCAD

![FreeCAD is a more complex tool ideal for precise design](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102756/freecad-is-a-more-complex-tool-ideal-for-precise-d-freecad-via-wikimedia-210809_download.jpg)

FreeCAD is a free open-source parametric CAD modeler and a good entry point for designers and mechanical engineers new to the 3D modeling world. Experts can still find this software well suited to advanced projects.

Parametric modeling allows a design to be updated by changing its dimensions and constraints, making FreeCAD particularly useful for precise, functional parts.

FreeCAD provides a wide range of specialized workbenches, including tools relevant to preparing models for 3D printing. It can import and export formats such as STL and OBJ, as well as engineering formats including STEP, IGES, and DWG.

The interface can feel cluttered and takes time to learn, but the software is completely free and open source, with no restrictions on how you use the models you create.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS, Linux
* **License Type:** Free Open Source
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Parametric 3D CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, STEP, IGES, DWG

### Vectary

![vectary CAD screenshot](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/25140528/vectaryscreenshot.png)

Vectary is a browser-based program that can help you accomplish a lot of design tasks. It touts itself as a combination of mesh modeling, subdivision modeling, and parametric design, offering a unique middle-ground positioning.

It lacks the manual control needed for mechanical parts but features intuitive free-form modeling and shaping tools. It includes a library of materials, lights, HDRI, and models or shapes, eliminating the need to look for assets every time. 

In the free tier, you can store ten projects online and export them as an OBJ or STL, making this a solid choice for working with a 3D printer.

* **Operating System:** Browser-based
* **License Type:** Free limited use (Freemium)
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Mesh, Subdivision & Parametric Hybrid
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ

### DesignSpark Mechanical

![DesignSpark Mechanical is a great option for those familiar with CAD software](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102805/designspark-mechanical-is-a-great-option-for-those-designsparkrs-via-youtube-210809_download.jpg)

DesignSpark Mechanical is best described as a hybrid direct modeler, allowing users to push and pull geometric features to make adjustments. It offers a toolset equivalent to popular CAD solutions on the market and resembles AutoCAD in some ways.

In the "Explorer" tier it is completely free, providing all required capabilities for 3D printing. Working with STEP and IGES formats requires paid tiers, but core functionalities include exporting models in STL format. It also comes with an expansive 3D model library featuring manufacturer-approved models.

* **Operating System:** Windows
* **License Type:** Free limited use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Direct Modeling
* **Key Export Formats:** STL

### Fusion

![Fusion 360 free license is as close as any other free CAD software will get to professional tools](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102808/fusion-360-free-license-is-as-close-as-any-other-f-autodesk-210809_download.jpg)

Fusion allows for parametric, free-form, surface, and mesh modeling and can export STLs for 3D printing. It is one of the most popular CAD options for hobbyists, bringing tools and features comparable to high-end solutions like SolidWorks.

Autodesk offers a free personal-use license of the software for non-commercial use. This license includes the same design and modeling tools as paid versions, which are more than enough for designing printable 3D models.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS
* **License Type:** Free for personal / non-commercial use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Parametric, Surface, Mesh & Free-form CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP

### Meshmixer

![Meshmixer is a 3D sculpting program focused solely on modeling for 3D printing](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102801/meshmixer-is-a-3d-sculpting-software-focused-solel-peopoly-210809_download.jpg)

Meshmixer works directly with polygon mesh 3D files in formats like STL and OBJ. It is a popular tool for analyzing, editing, repairing, cutting, hollowing, and modifying existing STL meshes, as well as generating manual or automatic support structures.

Making models from scratch is also possible via 3D sculpting techniques, making it ideal for organic shapes like characters and faces.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS
* **License Type:** Free to use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Mesh Sculpting & Mesh Editing
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, MIX

### Onshape

![Onshape is a superior CAD tool that is browser-based only with options for free licenses](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102811/onshape-is-a-superior-cad-tool-that-is-browser-bas-onshape-210809_download.jpg)

Onshape is a web-based platform that allows for parametric modeling, team collaboration, and data management, with a primary strength in technical and spare parts design.

Onshape offers a free tier for hobbyists and makers under the condition that created documents are shared publicly in a common user library.

* **Operating System:** Browser-based
* **License Type:** Free to use (Public documents)
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Cloud Parametric CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, STEP, IGES

### Dune 3D

![Image of: Dune 3D](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/21132944/dune3d.png)

Dune 3D is an open-source parametric CAD modeler aimed at users who want to design precise mechanical parts without the complexity of larger packages like FreeCAD. 

The software supports constrained 2D and 3D sketches, extrusions, fillets, chamfers, and reusable patterns. STEP models can be imported as references, while finished designs can be exported as STEP or STL files.

* **Operating System:** Windows, Linux
* **License Type:** Free Open Source
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Parametric 3D CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STEP, STL, DXF

### OpenSCAD

![OpenSCAD can model and simulate relatively complex designs](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/24153755/openscad-can-model-and-simulate-relatively-complex-adam-purdie-via-medium-221120_download.jpg)

OpenSCAD uses a dedicated scripting language rather than a visual click-and-drag interface to build models. This code-based method enables precise control over measurements and allows models to be altered quickly via script variables.

It is useful for functional 3D-printed objects such as brackets, adapters, enclosures, and repeated geometric components.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS, Linux
* **License Type:** Free Open Source
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Code-based CSG / Scripted Parametric Modeling
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OFF, AMF, DXF

## Advanced Software Options

> Pro-Tip: Parametric modeling software relies heavily on structured sketch dimensions. Define variables early in your workflow so you can scale mounting holes, wall thicknesses, and tolerances dynamically.

### Blender

![Blender is a complex software that make miracles, including 3D modeling for 3D printing](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/31102815/blender-is-a-complex-software-that-make-miracles-heavypoly-via-youtube-210809_download.jpg)

Blender is a 3D computer graphics suite capable of polygon mesh modeling, sculpting, and node-based procedural workflows.

While it lacks the strict dimensional constraint workflows of traditional mechanical CAD tools, its extensive feature set makes it an exceptional choice for artistic models, figurines, and highly complex organic structures.

* **Operating System:** Windows, macOS, Linux
* **License Type:** Free Open Source
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Mesh Modeling, Sculpting & Procedural Geometry Nodes
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, FBX

### Salome

![salome CAD screenshot](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/25133545/salomescreenshot.png)

Salome is an open-source parametric CAD modeler and multi-algorithm mesh generator. Using its Geometry and SHAPER modules, users can build dimension-controlled models suitable for technical applications.

In addition to STL output for 3D printing, Salome includes advanced modules for data analysis, meshing, and engineering simulation.

* **Operating System:** Windows, Linux
* **License Type:** Free Open Source
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Parametric CAD & Simulation Meshing
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, STEP, IGES

### Solid Edge Community Edition

![solid edge screenshot](https://i.all3dp.com/workers/images/fit=scale-down,w=1200,quality=79,gravity=0.5x0.5,format=auto/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/25165249/solidedgescreenshot.png)

Solid Edge Community Edition is a free version of Siemens’ industrial CAD software intended for makers, hobbyists, and personal non-commercial projects.

It includes advanced features for designing parts and complex assemblies, with integrated tools to streamline the additive manufacturing and 3D printing workflow.

* **Operating System:** Windows, Linux, macOS
* **License Type:** Free for personal / non-commercial use
* **Primary Modeling Type:** Industrial Parametric & Direct CAD
* **Key Export Formats:** STL, STEP, IGES, 3MF

## Summary & Final Recommendation

Choosing the best free CAD tool depends directly on your project requirements and modeling background:

* **For Absolute Beginners & Educators:** **Tinkercad** and **3D Slash** offer the gentlest learning curves without requiring software installation or engineering background knowledge.
* **For Functional Parts & Engineering:** Tools like **FreeCAD**, **Fusion**, **Onshape**, and **Solid Edge Community Edition** provide robust parametric sketching, dimensional constraints, and step-by-step revision histories essential for mechanical accuracy.
* **For Programmers & Algorithmic Designs:** **OpenSCAD** stands out as the premier code-based modeling tool, allowing exact mathematical definitions and scriptable parametric changes.
* **For Organic Sculpting & Artistic Models:** **Blender** and **Meshmixer** excel at working directly with polygon meshes, surface sculpting, and mesh repair rather than strict dimensional CAD geometry.
